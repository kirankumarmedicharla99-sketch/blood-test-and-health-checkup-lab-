PATIENT PREPARATION & REPORTS

Frequently Asked Diagnostic Questions

Clinician-verified answers regarding fasting rules, home sample collection hygiene, report turnaround times, and reference intervals.

Overnight fasting (10–12 hours) is required for Fasting Blood Glucose (FBS), Complete Lipid Profile (especially Triglycerides and LDL), Fasting Insulin, and Iron Studies. During the fasting window, do not consume food, tea, coffee, milk, juice, or chewing gum. Plain drinking water in moderate amounts is encouraged to keep your veins hydrated.
Routine hematology, biochemistry, lipid, thyroid, and diabetes panels (including CBC, LFT, KFT, HbA1c, and TSH) are processed on automated analyzers and released within 6 to 12 hours on the same day. You receive an encrypted link via WhatsApp and email, and can also view historical trends in the Patient Portal.
Our certified phlebotomists use sterile, single-use vacuum needles and color-coded vacutainers (EDTA, SST Gel, Fluoride) that are barcoded immediately at your bedside. Samples are placed inside calibrated 2°C–8°C insulated cold-chain carriers with digital temperature loggers and delivered directly to our Jubilee Hills central laboratory.
Essential blood pressure or heart medications can usually be taken with a sip of plain water unless your physician instructs otherwise. For thyroid hormone monitoring (T3, T4, TSH), clinicians typically recommend having your blood drawn in the morning before taking your daily levothyroxine tablet for consistent trough comparison.
Yes. Every comprehensive checkup package and critical result includes a complimentary tele-consultation with our clinical pathology team to explain what your biomarker values mean in context and help you prepare questions for your treating physician.
