Complete Blood Count (CBC) & Hematology

Our Complete Blood Count (CBC) with ESR is an essential 24-parameter hematological evaluation that provides a comprehensive overview of your circulating blood cells. Utilizing automated 5-part differential flow cytometry, our laboratory accurately quantifies red blood cells, hemoglobin concentrations, white blood cell subpopulations, and platelet indices.

Whether ordered for an annual preventive health checkup, fatigue evaluation, infection screening, or chronic hematological monitoring, every specimen is verified under 3-level internal quality control and reviewed by our clinical pathology team.
