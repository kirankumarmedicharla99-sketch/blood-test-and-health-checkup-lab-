PATIENT CARE & PREPARATION GUIDELINES

Accurate Preparation for Precise Test Results

Following recommended fasting guidelines, sample collection protocols, and report interpretation ensures dependable diagnostic insights.

Fasting Protocols & Morning Collections

Certain diagnostic blood tests—including Fasting Blood Glucose, Lipid Profiles, and Complete Metabolic Panels—require an overnight fast (8 to 12 hours) to avoid dietary interference with baseline biochemical markers.

  • Fasting Duration: Fast for 8 to 12 hours prior to your scheduled blood draw, consuming no food or caloric beverages.
  • Hydration Encouraged: Drinking plain water is recommended to keep veins well-hydrated for a smoother blood draw.
  • Medications: Continue prescribed medications unless specifically instructed otherwise by your doctor.

Convenient Doorstep Phlebotomy

Experience safe, stress-free blood sample collection in the comfort of your home. Ideal for senior citizens, busy professionals, parents with young children, and regular chronic health monitoring.

  • Sterile Safety Kits: Individual, pre-packaged vacuum tubes and safety needles opened in your presence.
  • Cold-Chain Transport: Temperature-monitored specimen cooler boxes safeguard sample stability in transit.
  • Flexible Scheduling: Select your preferred morning time slot, available 7 days a week.

Interactive Digital Reports & Historical Tracking

Your diagnostic report includes standardized biological reference intervals, clear visual trend graphs across past tests, and clinician-ready downloadable summaries accessible via our secure portal.

  • Standardized Reference Ranges: Color-coded indicators for normal, borderline, and out-of-range values.
  • Encrypted Health Portal: Secure patient portal with verified access and digital PDF report downloads.
  • Physician Sharing: Easily email or forward digital reports directly to your consulting clinician.

Sterile Single-Use Kits

All blood collection needles, tubes, and safety lancets are 100% single-use and safely disposed of in biohazard containers.

Fast Report Turnaround

Routine hematology and metabolic test reports are delivered within 12 to 24 hours via secure online access and SMS notification.

Pathologist-Verified Accuracy

Critical results and abnormal test values undergo secondary clinical review by certified clinical pathologists prior to release.
