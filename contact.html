Home Collection & Laboratory FAQs

Answers to common patient questions regarding doorstep blood sample collection, fasting preparation, payment options, and corporate health checkups.

Once you submit your booking online or via WhatsApp (+91 98490 24000), our dispatch team assigns a certified phlebotomist for your chosen morning time slot. You receive an SMS with the phlebotomist's name and live tracking link. The phlebotomist arrives with a single-use sterile collection kit, labels your vacutainer tubes with a 2D barcode in front of you, and transports the specimens in a 2°C–8°C cold-chain box to our Jubilee Hills laboratory.

Home sample collection is completely complimentary for all Comprehensive Health Checkup Packages and orders above $50 (or 2+ family members booked together). For single routine blood tests, a nominal convenience fee of $15 covers sterile single-use consumables and temperature-monitored logistics.
