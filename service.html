PATIENT PREPARATION & LAB FAQS

Frequently Asked Diagnostic Questions

Everything you need to know regarding fasting requirements, home blood sample collection, report turnaround times, and online portal access.

Fasting Blood Glucose, Comprehensive Lipid Profiles, and Full Body Health Checkup packages require 8 to 12 hours of overnight fasting. During the fasting period, you should avoid all food, tea, coffee, juice, or caloric beverages. Drinking plain water is strongly encouraged, as proper hydration keeps veins full and makes blood collection smoother. Tests such as CBC, HbA1c, and Thyroid Profile can be performed without fasting.
Routine hematology tests like Complete Blood Count (CBC) and Fasting Blood Sugar are delivered within 6 to 12 hours on the same day. Comprehensive Lipid Profiles, Thyroid Function Panels, HbA1c, and Full Body Health Checkup packages are verified by our pathologists and released within 12 to 24 hours via secure email link and your online Patient Portal.
When you book a home collection slot (available daily from 6:30 AM to 11:30 AM), our certified phlebotomist arrives at your doorstep wearing PPE and carrying a sterile single-use BD Vacutainer draw kit. All needle seals are broken in your presence, tubes are barcoded immediately, and samples are transported to our main diagnostic lab inside temperature-monitored 2°C–8°C cold-chain boxes.
Do not discontinue prescribed blood pressure, cardiac, or chronic medications unless explicitly advised by your ordering physician. For thyroid hormone monitoring (TSH, T3, T4), clinicians often recommend having your blood drawn in the morning just before taking your daily levothyroxine dose. Always inform our phlebotomist of any supplements (such as Biotin) you are taking.
Yes. All diagnostic reports are archived securely in your AARA Patient Portal. You can log in at any time to download high-resolution signed PDF reports, compare biomarker trends across previous checkups, or forward your results directly to your consulting physician.
